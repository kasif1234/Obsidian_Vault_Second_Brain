## 1. Core facts about Qatar’s EV ecosystem

- Qatar has around 200–300 public EV charging stations today, with KAHRAMAA saying there are already over 300 fast chargers nationwide.[thepeninsulaqatar+1](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/21/09/2025/qatar-expands-ev-charging-infrastructure-with-200-stations-app)
    
- Under the Tarsheed Smart EV Charging Program, the plan is to reach about 1,000 public charging stations by 2030 (and some studies mention potential for 4,000 by 2035).[mot+1](https://www.mot.gov.qa/en/news/qatar-rapidly-advancing-transition-e-mobility-among-mena-countries-pwc-report)
    
- Public fast chargers installed by KAHRAMAA are mainly 50 kW, 100 kW and 150 kW units, typically charging a car to 80% in 20–40 minutes.[[thepeninsulaqatar](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/21/09/2025/qatar-expands-ev-charging-infrastructure-with-200-stations-app)]​
    
- EV sales are still small but rising fast: BEVs are projected to go from about 1.1% of new car sales in 2024 to around 14–15% by 2035, with PHEVs adding another 9–10%.[pwc+1](https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/media-centre/2025/pwc-middle-east-sets-out-roadmap-to-accelerate-qatar-electric-mobility-transformation.html)
    
- Qatar targets at least 10% of total vehicle sales to be EVs by 2030 and plans more than 1,000 public chargers by 2030 to support this.[[mot.gov](https://www.mot.gov.qa/en/news/qatar-rapidly-advancing-transition-e-mobility-among-mena-countries-pwc-report)]​

## 2. Tarsheed & KAHRAMAA – what they do

- Tarsheed is the national conservation and energy‑efficiency program run by KAHRAMAA (Qatar General Electricity and Water Corporation).[[km](https://www.km.qa/Tarsheed/Pages/Tarsheed.aspx)]​
    
- Tarsheed runs the Smart EV Charging Program and mobile app, which shows charger locations, real‑time availability, compatibility, and lets users start/stop sessions and see history.[passengerterminaltoday+1](https://www.passengerterminaltoday.com/news/parking/hamad-international-and-kahramaa-launch-ev-charging-stations-under-tarsheed-program.html)
    
- Nearly 200–300 charging stations are already integrated into this smart platform, covering Doha and northern Qatar, with expansion planned west and northwest.[[thepeninsulaqatar](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/21/09/2025/qatar-expands-ev-charging-infrastructure-with-200-stations-app)]​
    
- KAHRAMAA opened Qatar’s first solar‑powered EV charging station at Mesaimeer using 216 photovoltaic panels to charge vehicles from solar energy via battery storage.[[km](https://www.km.qa/Tarsheed/Pages/TarsheedDetails.aspx?ItemID=7)]​
    
- KAHRAMAA has installed fast chargers at locations like Hamad International Airport; the airport charger is 50 kW, serves two cars at once, and can give roughly 125 km in about 30 minutes.[[passengerterminaltoday](https://www.passengerterminaltoday.com/news/parking/hamad-international-and-kahramaa-launch-ev-charging-stations-under-tarsheed-program.html)]​
    

**Key gaps you can highlight**

- The focus is on number and power of chargers, grid integration, and solar, not on solving extreme‑heat impacts like parked battery drain and range loss.[mot+1](https://www.mot.gov.qa/en/news/qatar-rapidly-advancing-transition-e-mobility-among-mena-countries-pwc-report)
    
- There are pilots of solar‑carport charging, but no evidence that shaded/conditioned EV parking bays are a universal standard.[km+1](https://www.km.qa/Tarsheed/Pages/TarsheedDetails.aspx?ItemID=7)
    
- The Tarsheed app is smart for locations and load management, but it does not yet provide thermal‑aware routing, charger‑derate predictions, or priority shaded‑bay reservations.[passengerterminaltoday+1](https://www.passengerterminaltoday.com/news/parking/hamad-international-and-kahramaa-launch-ev-charging-stations-under-tarsheed-program.html)

## 4. Heat and EV performance – numbers to quote

(From Gulf and manufacturer/technical guidance that is directly relevant to Qatar’s 40–45°C summers.)

- High ambient temperatures force the battery’s thermal management system to run pumps and fans more often, increasing consumption and sometimes causing the vehicle to use energy while parked.[dubicars+1](https://www.dubicars.com/news/ev-batteries-dubai-summer-heat-guide.html)
    
- Studies and regional guides report that EVs can see around 25–30% increased energy use in very hot weather when air‑conditioning runs hard, translating into roughly 15–30% effective range loss.[gacgroup+1](https://www.gacgroup.com/en-kw/news/article/high-temps-impact-ev-performance-40)
    
- Fast charging in high heat pushes the battery towards temperature limits; to protect cells, the car or charger automatically **derates** (reduces power), so a nominal 100 kW charger may deliver far ## 5. How to talk about your three AI features in this context

Use the stats above to make each feature sound like a direct answer to a documented gap.

## 1) Thermal Aware Routing Score (TARS)

- Qatar has hot months where daytime temperatures sit around 40–45°C; this increases EV energy use by roughly 25–30%, cutting range 15–30%.[dubicars+1](https://www.dubicars.com/news/ev-batteries-dubai-summer-heat-guide.html)
    
- Today’s navigation in Qatar (Google Maps, Waze, OEM nav) optimizes mainly for distance or time, not for heat‑induced consumption.
    
- TARS uses **temperature, traffic, elevation, and AC load models** to estimate how much extra battery you’ll lose on different routes and then chooses a cooler, smoother, less congested path to arrive with a safer state of charge.
    
- You can reference that Qatar is investing in smart control centers and digital transport services, so an AI routing layer fits MoT’s smart‑mobility vision.[[zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/business/technology-and-telecom/qatars-mot-strategy-2025-30-with-125-projects-to-develop-mobility-industry-awl5sh0t)]​
    

## 2) Predictive Charger Derate Protection (PCDP)

- Public DC chargers in Qatar are 50–150 kW, but in extreme heat the battery or charger may derate to protect hardware, stretching a 30‑minute stop much longer.[gacgroup+1](https://www.gacgroup.com/en-kw/news/article/high-temps-impact-ev-performance-40)
    
- Today, drivers only discover throttling when they plug in and see low kW; there is no predictive information in existing apps.[passengerterminaltoday+1](https://www.passengerterminaltoday.com/news/parking/hamad-international-and-kahramaa-launch-ev-charging-stations-under-tarsheed-program.html)
    
- PCDP would ingest historical station performance, time‑of‑day temperature, and live occupancy, and then forecast likely real power (for example, “this 100 kW charger is likely to give only 25–40 kW at 3 pm today”).
    
- That lets users choose a different station or a cooler time window, reducing queueing and frustration and aligning with KAHRAMAA’s smart platform goals.[thepeninsulaqatar+1](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/21/09/2025/qatar-expands-ev-charging-infrastructure-with-200-stations-app)
    

## 3) Shaded Priority Smart Reservation

- Solar carports have already been demonstrated by KAHRAMAA’s Mesaimeer photovoltaic station, but not rolled out widely as a standard for all EV parking.[[km](https://www.km.qa/Tarsheed/Pages/TarsheedDetails.aspx?ItemID=7)]​
    
- Long parking in 40–45°C heat leads to parked energy losses and battery stress; currently there is no digital way for high‑risk vehicles (for example, taxis, ride‑hail, delivery fleets) to secure the few shaded or solar‑carport bays.[dubicars+1](https://www.dubicars.com/news/ev-batteries-dubai-summer-heat-guide.html)
    
- Your system reserves shaded bays for vehicles that most need protection, and can optionally power low‑energy cabin cooling from carport solar instead of from the car’s battery.
    
- This aligns with Qatar’s targets for more renewable energy (up to 5 GW solar capacity by 2035) and smarter use of infrastructure.[[mot.gov](https://www.mot.gov.qa/en/news/qatar-rapidly-advancing-transition-e-mobility-among-mena-countries-pwc-report)]​
