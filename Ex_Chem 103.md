==
| Anion                  | Group                   | Group test reagent | What you see (group test)             | Confirmatory test                            | Positive result                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CO3^2− (carbonate)** | Group I (acid-volatile) | Dilute acid (HCl)  | **Bubbles / effervescence** (CO2 gas) | Limewater test (if done)                     | Limewater turns **milky**                                              |
| **S^2− (sulfide)**     | Group I (acid-volatile) | Dilute acid (HCl)  | Gas released                          | Smell test (careful)                         | **Rotten egg smell** (H2S)                                             |
| **SO4^2− (sulfate)**   | Group II (Ba group)     | BaCl2              | **White ppt**                         | Add dilute HCl to ppt                        | Ppt **stays** (insoluble BaSO4)                                        |
| **PO4^3− (phosphate)** | Group II (Ba group)     | BaCl2              | **White ppt**                         | Add dilute HCl to ppt                        | Ppt **dissolves**                                                      |
| **Cl− (chloride)**     | Group III (Ag group)    | AgNO3              | Precipitate forms                     | Chlorine water + hexane                      | Used to distinguish halides (no strong colored hexane layer like Br/I) |
| **Br− (bromide)**      | Group III (Ag group)    | AgNO3              | Precipitate forms                     | Chlorine water + hexane                      | **Orange/brown** color in hexane layer                                 |
| **I− (iodide)**        | Group III (Ag group)    | AgNO3              | Precipitate forms                     | Chlorine water + hexane                      | **Violet/purple** color in hexane layer                                |
| **SCN− (thiocyanate)** | Group III (Ag group)    | AgNO3              | Precipitate forms                     | Add Fe3+                                     | **Blood-red complex**                                                  |
| **NO3− (nitrate)**     | Group IV (soluble)      | No main ppt test   | Stays dissolved                       | **Brown ring test**                          | **Brown ring** at interface                                            |
| **CH3COO− (acetate)**  | Group IV (soluble)      | No main ppt test   | Stays dissolved                       | Acidify / ester test (ethanol + acid + heat) | **Vinegar smell** or **fruity smell**                                  |
